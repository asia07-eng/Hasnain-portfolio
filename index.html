About the Host Institution

Established in 1959, the Pakistan American Cultural Center (PACC), Hyderabad, has been serving the community for over six decades, promoting English language education, cultural exchange, and educational development. The institution provides educational and language-learning opportunities through its English Language Program and other skill-development courses.

I was assigned to teach a Pre-Beginners English language class, delivering grammar lessons, facilitating speaking and listening activities, preparing classroom exercises, assessing student learning, and supporting learners in developing basic English communication skills.

I also observed experienced teachers, participated in professional discussions with institutional management, and organized a performance-based language-learning project.

Internship Objectives

  1. Apply theoretical knowledge of Applied Linguistics to practical English language instruction.
  2. Develop effective classroom management and instructional communication skills.
  3. Implement learner-centred and communicative teaching strategies.
  4. Assess students’ grammatical knowledge and language development.
  5. Reflect critically on classroom experiences and improve professional teaching practices.
  6. Build students’ confidence through collaborative and creative language-learning activities.

Featured Teaching Project

Performed on 15 October 2026

The Great Summer Load-Shedding

Integrating language, creativity, and performance

One of the most memorable achievements of my internship was organizing an English-language skit entitled The Great Summer Load-Shedding, performed by my Pre-Beginners students on 15 October 2026.

The project was designed to encourage practical English communication through an entertaining and culturally familiar situation. The story portrayed a Pakistani family’s humorous experiences during a summer electricity outage.

My role and responsibilities

As the class teacher, I contributed to the development of the script, distribution of student roles, dialogue preparation, pronunciation practice, rehearsal coordination, and final stage presentation. I also served as the narrator, allowing students to concentrate on their assigned characters and spoken dialogues.

Pedagogical objectives

  • Improve spoken English through meaningful dialogue.
  • Strengthen pronunciation, fluency, and expressive communication.
  • Encourage teamwork and collaborative learning.
  • Develop confidence in public speaking.
  • Apply classroom language learning to an authentic performance task.

Outcome and reflection

The final performance was successfully presented before an audience and received appreciation from the teaching faculty. For me, the most rewarding aspect was witnessing students confidently deliver their dialogues and participate in a shared creative achievement.

This experience demonstrated that language learning can extend beyond textbooks and examinations. Drama and performance can provide meaningful opportunities for students to practise English, overcome hesitation, and develop communicative competence.
